# Contributing to Reciplex

Thank you for your interest in Reciplex!

At this time, Reciplex is a personal project, and I am not actively
looking for outside maintainers or feature contributions to the main repository.

### Forks and Pull Requests

That said, you are absolutely welcome to **fork this repository** and modify it to fit your own needs!

If you've written something awesome that you think belongs upstream,
you are welcome to open a Pull Request. However, please keep in mind:

- This project runs on limited personal free time.
- I may be slow to review PRs, and I might choose not to merge them if they don't align with the project's current roadmap or scope.

If you decide to fork and build your own version, happy hacking!
